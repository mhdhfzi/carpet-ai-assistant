Portfolio project · live demo

An AI assistant that answers, quotes and books jobs for a small cleaning business.

Customers chat on the website or WhatsApp in English or Bahasa Melayu. The assistant answers from the business's own documents with citations, prices jobs from the real price list, checks free slots and books them after the customer confirms. The owner sees every booking and what customers ask in a dashboard.

Open the owner dashboard

Kilau Carpet Care is fictional (based in Seremban, Malaysia). Data resets every night, and the demo dashboard login is public, so please don't type real personal details into the chat.

Try asking

Clicking a question opens the chat with it typed in. The office question is out of scope on purpose: the assistant should hand it to the owner instead of guessing a price.

Answers with sources (RAG)

Business documents are chunked, embedded with Voyage AI and stored in Postgres with pgvector. Claude answers only from the retrieved passages and cites them; the owner sees the citations for every reply.

Tool calling, with a confirmation step

Prices, availability and bookings come from tools backed by tested business logic, never from the model's memory. A booking is only created after the customer confirms the summary in a later message.

Knows when to hand off

Low-confidence answers, upset customers and out-of-scope requests go to the owner, and every question the assistant couldn't answer is logged so the knowledge base can be improved.

Built like a production service

Owner login with Argon2 and JWT, rate limits, a daily AI cost cap, structured logs with request IDs, and CI/CD that tests every change and deploys main to Google Cloud Run.

Owner dashboard

Bookings with the chat that led to each one, plus analytics: common questions, chat-to-booking conversion, busy days and unanswered questions. Sign in with the read-only demo account: you can look at everything, and the server refuses any change.
