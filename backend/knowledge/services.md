# Services we offer

We are a cleaning business based in Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia. We clean carpets, sofas,
mattresses and cars.

## Carpet cleaning (cuci karpet)

We clean these types of carpet (jenis karpet):

- Karpet Biasa: standard, everyday carpet
- Karpet Shaggy: shaggy or long-pile carpet
- Karpet Velvet
- Karpet Premium Velvet
- Karpet Span
- Karpet Tatami
- Karpet Handmade: handmade carpet

Carpet cleaning is priced per square foot (kaki persegi), and the price per square foot
depends on the carpet type. Carpets are usually collected and cleaned at our shop rather
than at the customer's home.

## Handmade carpet (karpet handmade)

Handmade carpet is priced per square foot within a range. The exact price depends on the
carpet's material, so the owner inspects the carpet and confirms the final price. The
assistant can give a price range, but not an exact price, for handmade carpet.

## Sofa cleaning (cuci sofa)

Sofa cleaning is priced per seat. A three-seater sofa counts as three seats.

## Mattress cleaning (cuci tilam)

Mattress cleaning is priced per mattress, by size: single, queen or king.

## Car cleaning (cuci kereta)

We also clean cars. The price depends on the size of the car, for example sedan or MPV.
Car cleaning prices are confirmed by the owner.
