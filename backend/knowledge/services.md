# Services we offer

Cuci-Cuci Senawang cleans carpets (for homes, mosques, surau, halls and offices), mattresses,
sofas and car seats, serving all of Negeri Sembilan.

## Carpet cleaning (cuci karpet)

We clean these types of carpet (jenis karpet):

- Karpet Biasa: standard, everyday carpet
- Karpet Shaggy: shaggy or long-pile carpet
- Karpet Velvet
- Karpet Premium Velvet
- Karpet Span
- Karpet Tatami
- Karpet Handmade: handmade carpet

Carpet cleaning is priced per square foot (kaki persegi), and the price per square foot
depends on the carpet type. Carpets are usually collected and cleaned at our shop rather
than at the customer's home.

## Handmade carpet (karpet handmade)

Handmade carpet is priced per square foot within a range. The exact price depends on the
carpet's material, so the owner inspects the carpet and confirms the final price. The
assistant can give a price range, but not an exact price, for handmade carpet.

## Sofa cleaning (cuci sofa)

Sofa cleaning is priced per seat. A three-seater sofa counts as three seats.

## Mattress cleaning (cuci tilam)

Mattress cleaning is priced per mattress, by size: single, queen or king.

## Car seat cleaning (cuci seat kereta)

We clean a car's fabric seats, car carpet (karpet kereta) and dashboard. It is a cleaning
of the car interior, not a full car wash. The price depends on the size of the car, for
example sedan or MPV, and is confirmed by the owner.

## Mosque, surau and hall carpets (karpet masjid, surau, dewan)

We clean carpets for mosques, surau and halls. There is no fixed price: the owner visits
the site first to see the carpets, then gives a quote through WhatsApp. To arrange a site
visit, we need the location, roughly how much carpet there is, and a contact name and
phone number.

## Office and commercial carpets (karpet pejabat, kedai)

We clean carpets for offices, shops and other commercial premises. As with mosque carpets,
the owner visits the site first and then gives a quote through WhatsApp. To arrange a site
visit, we need the location, roughly how much carpet there is, and a contact name and
phone number.
