# Booking and opening hours

## Opening hours (waktu operasi)

We are open every day except Friday, from 9:00 am to 5:30 pm (Malaysia time). We are closed
on Fridays (hari Jumaat).

## Public holidays (cuti umum)

We are usually closed on public holidays.

## How much notice is needed to book

Bookings must be made at least 2 days in advance, so that we can arrange the schedule.
Same-day and next-day bookings are not available.

## How booking works

1. The customer tells us which services they need, the size or number of items, and their
   preferred date and time.
2. We record the booking as pending.
3. The owner contacts the customer to agree on the time.
4. Once the customer agrees, the booking is confirmed.

A booking is not final until the owner has confirmed the time with the customer.

## Visit times

Visits must start and finish within opening hours (9:00 am to 5:30 pm). We have one team,
so only one home visit can happen at a time. Customers who drop items off at our shop do not
need a visit slot.
