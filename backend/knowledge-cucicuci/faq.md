# Frequently asked questions

## Do you clean carpets at my house?

Carpets are usually cleaned at our shop. We collect the carpet from your home, or you can
drop it off at our shop, and we return it within 5 to 7 days.

## When will I get my carpet back?

Within 5 to 7 days after we collect it or after you drop it off at our shop.

## Why can the final carpet price differ from the estimate?

The estimate uses the size you give us. We measure the carpet after collecting it and
confirm the final price based on the measured size. For handmade carpet, the price also
depends on the material, which the owner checks.

## Can I bring my sofa or mattress to your shop?

Yes. Sofas and mattresses are usually cleaned at your home, but you can also bring them to
our shop.

## Can I book for today or tomorrow?

No. Bookings need at least 2 days' notice so that we can arrange the schedule.

## Are you open on Friday?

No. We are closed every Friday. We are open the other six days from 9:00 am to 5:30 pm.

## How do I book? Can I book in this chat?

Bookings are made on WhatsApp at 019-276 2890, not in this chat. Send your name, area, the
services you need, the size or number of items and your preferred pickup date, and the owner
replies with a quote and a pickup date. The booking form on our website also opens WhatsApp
with your details filled in.

## Do you charge for pickup and delivery?

Pickup and delivery are free within 15 km of our shop in Senawang. Beyond 15 km, there is an
extra transport charge, which the owner confirms.

## Where is your shop?

89-G, Jalan Bandar Senawang 5, Taipan 2, Pusat Bandar Senawang, 70450 Seremban, Negeri
Sembilan. You can also WhatsApp or call us at 019-276 2890.

## How much does mosque or office carpet cleaning cost?

There is no fixed price for mosque, surau, hall, office or shop carpets. The owner visits
the site first and then gives a quote through WhatsApp. Share the location, roughly how much
carpet there is, and your name and phone number, and the owner will arrange the visit.

## Do you clean car seats?

Yes. We clean fabric car seats, car carpet and the dashboard, usually at your home. The
price depends on the size of the car, and the owner confirms it.
